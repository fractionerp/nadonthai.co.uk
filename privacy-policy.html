If you do not agree with this privacy policy, you must not use this website or submit information to us through or in connection with this website. We are committed to ensuring that your privacy is protected. From time to time we may change this policy by updating this page.

1. Who we are

"Nadon Thai Restaurants" comprises three companies; Nadon 55 Ltd, Chester Nadon Ltd and Nadon Newcastle Ltd. This Privacy Policy sets out how we use and protect any information that you give us when you use our website.

2. What information we collect and how we use it

Our website allows visitors to contact us through enquiry and booking forms. We may collect personal information such as your name, email address and telephone number. We use this information to provide our services and to handle your booking, request or complaint.

If you choose to sign up for special offers, we collect your email address and use it to send promotional emails from Nadon Thai. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any promotional email.

3. Security

We take your privacy seriously. We have taken every appropriate measure to keep your data secure and confidential. However, please note that the transmission of data over the internet can never be guaranteed as entirely secure, and therefore any transmission of data is at your own risk.

4. Sharing information

We do not sell your personal information. We may provide it to trusted service providers that process information on our behalf, including our booking providers and Brevo, our email marketing provider. They may only process your information to provide their services to us. We may also disclose information where we have a legal obligation to do so.

5. Your rights

You have a right to access the personal information we hold about you, and to receive a copy in a commonly accessible format, free of charge. You have a right to transfer that information.

You have a right to ensure any inaccurate or incomplete information is corrected or removed. You may request that we cease processing your personal information, while continuing to store it. You may also request that your personal information is removed from our records.

You can withdraw your consent to promotional emails at any time by using the unsubscribe link in an email or by contacting us.

6. Cookies and analytics

Cookies and similar browser storage can remember preferences and help services work. We use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google LLC, only if you choose to accept analytics. It measures site visits, clicks on restaurant booking links and visits to booking pages so we can improve the site. We also measure visits to the Morpeth booking-request confirmation page. Information sent to Google may include pages visited, device and browser information, and referral information. Google processes IP addresses to derive approximate location information and discards them before logging analytics data; GA4 does not store individual IP addresses. We do not enable Google Signals or advertising personalisation in this website's analytics tag.

Google Analytics uses cookies such as _ga and _ga_DK1PX0XQ3B to distinguish browsers and maintain session information. Their default lifetime is up to two years and may be renewed on later visits. For details of Google's processing, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

We store your analytics choice in your browser's local storage under nadon-analytics-consent-v1 for six months. This preference is used only to remember your choice. When analytics is accepted, a booking click may also be held temporarily in the current tab's session storage so it can be recorded after navigation; it is removed after processing or on the next page, and is only eligible to be sent for 15 seconds. You can accept, reject or withdraw consent using Analytics settings in the footer. Rejecting analytics does not stop you using the site or booking a table. Withdrawing consent stops our Google Analytics tag and removes its accessible first-party analytics cookies; it does not delete information already collected by Google.

Our Newcastle and Durham booking pages also embed resOS, a separate third-party service. Its booking, security and other technologies may use cookies, including AWSALBCORS and __obref. resOS manages its own cookie and consent settings; the analytics choice on this website controls our Google Analytics tag, not the resOS embed. See the resOS privacy policy for its processing information. External delivery and gift voucher sites apply their own policies when you visit them.

7. Links to other websites

Our website may contain links to other websites of interest. However, once you have used these links to leave our site, you should note that we do not have any control over that other website. Therefore, we cannot be responsible for the protection and privacy of any information which you provide whilst visiting such sites, as such sites are not governed by this Privacy Statement. You should exercise caution and look at the Privacy Statement applicable to the website in question.
